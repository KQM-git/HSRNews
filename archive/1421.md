# Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game V4.6 Update Details
## Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game V4.6 Update Details
![Banner](https://sdk.hoyoverse.com/upload/ann/2026/09/16/dd7a84cb210898d56d1b610646895ca0_8011190875223903025_transformed.png)

After the Version 4.6 update, Double Ascension Point mode will be available in "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game," speeding up your acquisition of Ascension Level Rewards!

### New Content

- Double Ascension Points Acquisition

After this update, complete "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" matches to get double Ascension Points.

### "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" Season Ending Soon

After Version 4.6 ends, some rewards in "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" will be reset. The rewards you haven't claimed will become unclaimable, so Trailblazers should take care to get their rewards in time.

### Reset Details

- After Version 4.6 ends, some rewards/content in "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" will be reset. The rewards/content to be reset include:

"Bond Link" Related Rewards

"Data Bank" Related Rewards and Collection Progress

"Ascension Level" Related Rewards and Level

"Standard Gambit" Related Rank Rewards

"Expected Profit" Related Rewards

"Seasonal Advantage" Related Nodes and Activation Items

Winning Streak in "Currency Wars"

※ If your winning streak is ≥ 10 at the end of the season, it will be recorded in the description details of the "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" commemorative trophy.

- Rewards/content that will not be reset include:

"Startup Guide" Related Rewards

Activated Nodes in Permanent Advantage

※ The Trailblazer's rank in the "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game" season will affect their starting rank in the new season.

※ In "Currency Wars: Zero-Sum Game," the "Final Victor" Avatar will be moved to Herta's Store and can be exchanged with Herta Bonds. The relevant item has a holding limit, and cannot be exchanged once the limit is reached.
