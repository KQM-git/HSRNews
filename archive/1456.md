## Version 4.7 Store Update
![Banner](https://sdk.hoyoverse.com/upload/ann/2026/10/18/863367ff4a5539e0067a0b8bb67e048f_4543358197366884811_transformed.png)

Dear Trailblazers,

After the Version 4.7 update, **value packs** will be available for a limited time! ■ Event Period

After the Version 4.7 update – 2026/12/23 03:59:00 (global)

■ Event Rewards

![img](https://sdk.hoyoverse.com/upload/ann/2026/10/29/bf6f08afebce397d88c7b1034e80e67a_7101089341627310854_transformed.png)

■ Special Notes

- During the event, when your total Resonance reaches 1,600 and 3,200, you can obtain Affinitive Eidolon ×1 respectively.

- During the event, consume Star Rail Special Pass ×1 in any Event Warp to obtain Resonance ×16.

- During the event, consume Fashion Invite ×1 in the "Phantasia Showstage" event to obtain Resonance ×10.

- If the event rewards are obtained through irregular means, the Express Crew will hold the authority to take measures such as deducting the event rewards.
